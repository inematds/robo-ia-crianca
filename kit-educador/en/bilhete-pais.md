# Note for parents

Copy this into your child’s planner, print it, or send it to the class WhatsApp group.

**Today we talked about AI robots**

Hello, families! Today the class learned that an AI robot is like an **ear connected to the internet**:
it records what a child says and sends it to a company’s computer, which replies.

Many low-cost robots sold online for children **speak only a few languages (in the listings we checked: Mandarin, English and Cantonese)** and send voice recordings to AI companies outside the country. Some have cameras.

**Before buying one (or if you already have one at home), check 3 things:**
1. Does it speak your language?
2. Does it work without internet?
3. Is it camera-free?

If it fails any one of these checks, it’s better not to buy it. If you already bought one: connect the robot to a **guest Wi-Fi network**,
cover the camera, keep it **out of the bedroom at night**, and use it **with an adult nearby**.

The children agreed: **don’t tell a robot your full name, school, address, or password.**

Full guide and printable checklist (free):
https://inematds.github.io/robo-ia-crianca/guia/en/
