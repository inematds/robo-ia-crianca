Before using an AI robot

It's an ear connected to the internet. Ask:

1
Does it speak your language?
If it doesn't, it wasn't made for you.
2
Does it work without internet?
If it doesn't, everything you say leaves your home.
3
Does it have a camera?
If it does, it may send pictures of your home.
Not your language, needs the internet, or has a camera? Talk to an adult before using it.
  • I don't tell a robot: my full name, school, address, or password.
  • Robots stay out of the bedroom at night.
  • Weird question? I call an adult.

Today we talked about AI robots

Hello, families! The class learned that an AI robot is like an ear connected to the internet: it records what a child says and sends it to a company's computer, which replies. Many cheap robots sold online for children speak only a few languages (in the listings we checked: Mandarin, English and Cantonese) and send their voice to AI companies outside the country. Some have a camera.

Before buying one (or if you already have one at home), check:

  1. Does it speak your language?
  2. Does it work without internet?
  3. Is it camera-free?

One answer is no? It's better not to buy it. If you've already bought one: use guest Wi-Fi, cover the camera, keep it out of the bedroom at night, and have an adult nearby during use. The children agreed: don't tell a robot your full name, school, address, or password.
